Welcome to Aestimanda

About

Aestimanda is an open-access, web-based research environment for exploring Greek and Sanskrit annotated corpora. The annotated corpora of Vedic Sanskrit and Homeric Greek. The platform provides an intuitive interface for searching and exploring linguistic data from both corpora.

Users can search by lemma, word form, or location, and refine results using grammatical and semantic filters. Search results include contextual passages, translations, and detailed linguistic annotations, making it easy to explore individual occurrences and compare patterns across the corpus.
