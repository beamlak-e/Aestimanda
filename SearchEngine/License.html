RigVeda

  • The Rigveda text used in this project is based on the digital edition prepared by Karen Thomson and Jonathan Slocum,
  • available through the Linguistics Research Center at University of Texas at Austin. The digital text is based on:
  • van Nooten, Barend A., and Gary B. Holland, editors. Rigveda: A Metrically Restored Text. Harvard University Press, 1994.

Homer

  • The Homeric Greek text was extracted from the Perseus Digital Library.
  • The edition used is: Homeri Opera. Edited by David B. Monro and Thomas W. Allen.
  • Oxford Classical Texts. Oxford University Press, 1920.
